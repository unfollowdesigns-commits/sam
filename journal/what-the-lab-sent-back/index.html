What the lab sent back

2 April 2022 1 min read35mm · process · Latent

Everything in Latent is a mistake. Light got in somewhere it should not have, and the lab handed back five rolls of arms and shoulders with red burned across them.

The first instinct is to throw the roll out. I have thrown out better frames than these for less. What stopped me was that the leak fell across the arm in a way I could not have arranged if I had spent the afternoon trying.

Film keeps a record of what happened to it, not only of what you pointed it at.

So the series is the accidents I decided to keep. That is the whole editorial principle, and I do not pretend it is more rigorous than that.

The scans are untouched

Nothing here has been graded back towards what the frame was supposed to look like. The green cast is the cast the lab returned. The only decisions after the fact were which frames to keep and what order to put them in.

It is worth saying because the question comes up: no, this is not a filter, and no, I cannot reproduce it on request.
