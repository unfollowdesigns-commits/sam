      Who is Sam?
      Sam is a photographer based in Tunis, Tunisia, working mainly on 35mm film. He has photographed in Tunis and London since 2018.
      What kind of photography does Sam shoot?
      Portraiture, fashion and jewellery campaigns, experimental colour film, and black and white street photography.
      What does Sam shoot on?
      Mostly 35mm film, with some digital work. Scans are published as the lab returned them rather than graded back towards a corrected image.
      Where is Sam based?
      Tunis, Tunisia. Much of the black and white work was made in London between 2021 and 2022.
      Is Sam available for commissions?
      Yes — editorial, portrait and fashion commissions, and prints. Enquiries by email.
